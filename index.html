How to play

  • Move: A/D or left/right arrows; touch players use the left joystick.
  • Jump: Space for a fixed-height jump and one airborne double jump; the touch JUMP button does the same.
  • Climb: W/up to grip the background, then use directions to climb; Space drops. Up does not jump.
  • Drill: Hold Return or DRILL to cut a narrow tunnel in the aimed direction; untouched ground drills slowly.
  • Combat: X or SHOOT fires in combat courses; jumping onto a patrol also defeats it.
  • Course features: Collect every glowing checkpoint to unlock the checkered exit; avoid flying patrols, collect lightning speed boosts and mushrooms, and ride the optional moving platforms.
  • Reset: Reset run restores the selected photo course without saving an abandoned attempt.
